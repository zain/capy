# Code of conduct

We want Capy to be a useful place for founders, developers, and people who work with equity records.

- Be respectful and specific. Critique the code or idea, not the person.
- Make room for different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, post sexual content, or publish another person's private information.
- Keep reports and examples free of customer data and credentials.
- Respect decisions to stop a discussion or move sensitive material into a private channel.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

Report conduct concerns to [hello@capyinc.com](mailto:hello@capyinc.com). The maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban accounts depending on the severity and context. Reports are handled privately as far as practical; the maintainers may need to share relevant details to investigate or act.
